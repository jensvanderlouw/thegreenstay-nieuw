Where we create value

The concept touches three axes: economic, social and environmental. For each axis we looked at what it delivers for the parties involved.

Economic

Lower cleaning costs

Fewer cleans carried out means fewer hours worked per stay.

A reward for the guest

Credits the guest can spend, for example at the bar.

Extra spending

Credits can be the reason to order something that otherwise would not have been ordered.

Social

The guest decides

The guest keeps control over their own room and comfort.

Collaboration between departments

Reception, housekeeping and bar have to share the same information for it to work.

A visible contribution

The guest sees what their choice delivers, which increases involvement.

Environmental

Less laundry

Towels and bed linen are washed less often.

Less water and fewer products

Every skipped clean saves clean water and cleaning products.

Less energy

Less vacuuming, airing and heating during cleaning.

How we would measure this

The returns are only credible when they are measured instead of assumed. Our proposal is to track per property:

  • Number of skipped cleans and refreshes per period
  • Water, energy and cleaning product use per occupied room
  • Kilograms of laundry washed
  • Credits spent and credits left unused
  • Guest satisfaction, separately for participants and non-participants

The figures in this concept have not been measured yet. We have worked out the research design with interview guides for guests and hotel staff, but the data collection still has to happen.
