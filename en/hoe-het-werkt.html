How it works

Five steps, from arrival to departure. The guest stays in control at every moment and can undo an earlier choice without explanation.

  1. On arrival

    At check-in the guest hears what TheGreenStay is and what it delivers. Joining is a choice, not a default setting.

  2. The daily choice

    Each day the guest indicates whether the room needs cleaning, or whether a refresh is enough (towels, waste, refills).

  3. Housekeeping sees it immediately

    The choice sits in the system that reception, housekeeping and the bar share, so no room is skipped or cleaned unnecessarily.

  4. Credits are added

    For every skipped clean, an agreed amount goes to the guest's credits. The balance is visible at all times.

  5. On departure

    The guest spends the credits, for example at the bar, or lets them expire. There are no hidden conditions.

Choice options per day

Three options, so it does not become all or nothing.

ChoiceWhat happensCredits
Full cleanStandard service, as alwaysnone
RefreshTowels, waste and consumablespartial
No serviceRoom stays untouchedfull

We determine the exact credit amounts from the real cost per clean. We deliberately left them out here, because a figure pulled out of thin air makes the whole concept unbelievable.

What the guest needs in order to trust it

  • Clear explanation in plain language, without sustainability jargon
  • A choice that takes one action to change
  • A credit balance that is always visible
  • The certainty that cleaning stays available when it is requested
  • No difference in service quality between joining and not joining
