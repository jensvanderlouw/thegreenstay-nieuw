The concept

Worked out by the five of us, from the first brainstorm to this site.

Five people, five roles

Our roles were not handed out randomly. Each of us got the role that fits where that person is naturally strong, so the task moves the team forward instead of just hanging off it.

What characterises us as a group is that we start quickly and have a lot of trust in each other. What we are working on is slowing down: listening better, pausing more often before a decision, and looking at a problem from more than one side.

What we researched

For the research we wrote two interview guides: one for guests and one for the staff of the hotel we visited. With those we want to check whether the needs we identified on paper also come back in practice.

  • A short prototype description as the starting point
  • Testing population and sample
  • Interview guide for guests and for hotel staff
  • Data collection and qualitative analysis
  • Evaluation and improvement of the prototype

About this site

TheGreenStay is a student project. The hotel concept, the figures and the analyses were made for a school assignment and have not been tested in practice. It is not an existing company or an existing product.
