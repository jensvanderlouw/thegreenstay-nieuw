Where value can be destroyed

A concept that promises value can also destroy it. We mapped four ways in which TheGreenStay can derail, who is affected by each, and which measure should prevent it.

The four risks

Economic

The reward is greater than the saving

If the credits cost more than the hotel saves, the concept does not pay for itself and the hotel is paying for its own sustainability story.

Social

Unclear choice or an error in the credits

If the guest does not understand what they are saying yes or no to, or the balance is wrong, trust falls and satisfaction falls with it.

Social

Incorrect credit balance

A wrong balance affects both the guest and the staff who have to resolve the questions at the front desk.

Environmental

Unproven green claims

If the environmental benefit cannot be demonstrated, that damages environmental trust and ultimately trust in the hotel as a whole.

Risk, who it affects, what we do about it

RiskWho is affectedMeasure
Reward exceeds the savingThe hotelMeasure the cost per clean before setting credits
Unclear choiceThe guestExplicit consent and a choice that is easy to change
Incorrect credit balanceGuest and staffOne shared digital record that all departments see the same way
Unproven green claimEnvironment and trustMeasure actual consumption instead of calculating it in advance

What we take from this

The analysis shows that the biggest threat is not technical but relational. Almost every risk comes down to an expectation that is not met: the guest expects an accurate bill, honest information and the same service as always.

That is why the first rule is that cleaning always stays available and the choice is always clear. A concept that makes the guest feel they have to give something up loses the guest — and then the environmental gain disappears too.

More on how the choice works in practice is on the page about how it works.

The risk in one image

The fingerprint stands for the most underestimated risk in the table: personal data that is wrong. A credit balance is nothing more than a promise on a screen, and the moment that promise does not match what the guest believes they earned, the trust is gone.

That is why the shared digital record is not a technical side note but the core of the measure.
